# Product Requirements Documents (PRDs)

Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) are formal documents that outline the features, functionality, and purpose of a product or a specific feature within a product. They serve as a central reference point for all stakeholders involved in the product development lifecycle.

## Key Components of a PRD:

*   **Introduction:** Overview of the product/feature, its purpose, and target audience.
*   **Goals & Objectives:** What the product/feature aims to achieve, often tied to business metrics.
*   **User Stories/Use Cases:** Detailed descriptions of how users will interact with the product.
*   **Functional Requirements:** Specific behaviors and capabilities of the system.
*   **Non-Functional Requirements:** Performance, security, usability, and other quality attributes.
*   **Scope:** What is included and excluded from the current development phase.
*   **Assumptions, Constraints, & Dependencies:** Factors that might impact development.
*   **Success Metrics:** How the success of the product/feature will be measured.

## Purpose:

*   To ensure a shared understanding of the product among all teams (product, design, engineering, QA).
*   To guide the development process and prioritize features.
*   To serve as a basis for testing and validation.
*   To track progress and manage changes throughout the product lifecycle.

## How it relates to this project:

For this project, PRDs will be used to clearly define the requirements for new features and enhancements, ensuring that development efforts are aligned with product goals and user needs.
