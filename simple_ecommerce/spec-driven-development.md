# Spec-Driven Development

Spec-driven development (SDD) is an approach where specifications are the primary artifacts driving the development process. These specifications serve as a single source of truth for requirements, design, and testing.

## Key Principles:

*   **Clear and Executable Specifications:** Specifications are precise, unambiguous, and often machine-readable.
*   **Collaboration:** Encourages close collaboration between product owners, designers, and developers.
*   **Automated Testing:** Specifications can be directly used to generate or validate automated tests.
*   **Early Feedback:** Issues are identified early in the development cycle.

## Benefits:

*   Improved communication and understanding among stakeholders.
*   Reduced ambiguities and misinterpretations.
*   Higher quality software due to comprehensive testing.
*   Faster development cycles by minimizing rework.

## How it relates to this project:

This project aims to incorporate principles of spec-driven development to ensure that features are built precisely to defined requirements and are thoroughly tested.
